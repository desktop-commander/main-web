Good to know

Your work.
Your context.

Context Commander saves project information you choose to submit, rather than automatically importing all your conversations.

Do I need Desktop Commander installed?

No. Context Commander is a separate hosted MCP for project memory. It does not require access to your computer or execute commands.

Can I use it in a different AI conversation?

Yes. Retrieve the same saved project from a new conversation using your account and an authorized, compatible MCP client. Tool availability and automatic retrieval depend on the AI application.

What exactly is stored?

Project names, goals, tasks, decisions, progress summaries, blockers and next steps submitted to Context Commander. Checkpoints describe reported progress; they do not verify that work was completed.

Can I delete my saved context?

Yes. Use the project deletion tool or contact support to request deletion or export. Contact support for details. The product-specific privacy policy will be available before launch.
