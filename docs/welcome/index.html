Remote MCP

Want more? Try Remote.

Desktop Commander Remote connects your computers to AI apps on the web and on your phone. Same tools as the local one: files, terminal and processes.

  • Use it from your phone

    Ask ChatGPT or Claude on your phone. The work happens on your computer at home or at the office.

  • Control several computers and servers

    Connect laptops, desktops, servers and VMs, then tell the AI which one to work on.

Use cases

What people use Remote for

Jobs Remote users describe to us, done in the chat they already have open.

  • Keep working from your phone

    Start a long job at your desk, then check on it, steer it or collect the result from your phone while you are out.

  • One chat, several machines

    Split a big batch job across a Linux box and three Windows PCs, all from one ChatGPT conversation.

  • Fix the computer, not just the code

    Set up SSH, change power settings, find what filled the disk. The AI checks the real machine, makes the change, then confirms it worked.

  • No more remote-desktop copy and paste

    Work with files, PowerShell and logs on a remote Windows PC straight from chat, instead of remoting in and pasting commands back and forth.

Setup

How it works

Set it up once, in a few minutes.

01

Sign up

Create an account on mcp.desktopcommander.app with your email.

mcp.desktopcommander.app 01 / 04
Create your account
you@company.com
Continue with email Account ready
02

Connect a device

Add the computer you want to reach: a laptop, a desktop, or a server. Connect as many as you need.

mcp.desktopcommander.app 02 / 04
Devices Add device
  • MacBook Pro online
  • Home PC online
  • vps-fra1 connecting online
03

Connect your AI client

Install the plugin in ChatGPT, or add the connector in Claude. Any other MCP client works too.

Connectors 03 / 04
  • ChatGPT Install the plugin
  • Claude Add the connector
  • Any MCP client mcp.desktopcommander.app/mcp
04

Start chatting

Ask for what you want done. Your assistant works on the machine and reports back.

New chat 04 / 04
Put the October invoices in one folder.
Desktop Commander 2 tool calls
start_search invoice 14 files
move_file Finance/2026-10 14 moved

Done. All 14 October invoices are in Finance/2026-10.
