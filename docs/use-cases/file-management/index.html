File chores shouldn't take a dozen tools.

Converting a photo means one website. Renaming a batch means another app. Sorting a messy folder means an afternoon of dragging files around. Each small job eats time you would rather spend on the work itself.

Work with your files directly

Desktop Commander connects ChatGPT or Claude to the files on your computer, so one message replaces the downloading, renaming, and dragging you would do by hand.

"Find all invoices from Q3 and move them to my Accounting folder." Desktop Commander finds them and moves them for you.

How it works

Set it up once, in a few minutes.

1

Sign up

Create an account on mcp.desktopcommander.app with your email.

2

Connect a device

Add the computer you want to reach: a laptop, a desktop, or a server. Connect as many as you need.

3

Connect your AI client

Install the plugin in ChatGPT, or add the connector in Claude. Any other MCP client works too.

4

Start chatting

Ask for what you want done. Your assistant works on the machine and reports back.
